# Privacy Policy for Vivianne GUI

**Last updated:** [Date]

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the Vivianne GUI (the "Application"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Application. It applies to the Application running on desktop via Tauri, and any associated website.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage data:** We may automatically collect information about how the Application is accessed and used, including but not limited to: IP address, browser type, operating system, the time and date of your access, and the pages you visit within the Application.
- **Diagnostic data:** Through Tauri, we may collect crash logs, performance statistics, and diagnostic data to help us maintain, improve, and secure the Application.

### 2.2 Information You Provide
- **Account information:** If you create an account or sign in, we may collect your email address, username, and password (hashed).
- **Content you input:** Any text, files, or prompts you send to the AI assistant within the Application may be processed by the underlying AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google). We do not store your raw input beyond what is necessary to provide the service.
- **Feedback:** If you submit feedback or bug reports, we may store your comments and contact information to respond to you.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide, maintain, and improve the Application and its AI features.
- To personalize your experience within the Application.
- To monitor and address technical issues, crashes, and security vulnerabilities.
- To communicate with you about updates, features, or support matters.
- To comply with legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of the Application and its users.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

- **AI service providers:** When you send a prompt to the AI assistant, it is transmitted to a third‑party AI provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) in accordance with that provider's privacy policy. We do not sell your data to unrelated third parties.
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid government request.
- **Service providers:** We may engage third‑party service providers to host, debug, or improve the Application. These providers are bound by confidentiality and use‑limitation obligations.

## 5. Data Retention

- Usage diagnostics and crash logs are retained for [30] days unless a longer period is required for security or legal reasons.
- Account information is retained for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the service.
- Content you input is not stored long‑term; it is typically processed in real time and not persisted beyond the session unless you explicitly save it.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you.
- Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
- Object to or restrict processing of your data.
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at [privacy@vivianne.example.com].

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and, where appropriate, by sending a notification within the Application.
