# Vivianne YouTube video: design language

Date: 2026-10-29
Scope: the YouTube video, its posters, and every animation and motion graphic made for it.

## Decision

The whole video uses **Frutiger Aero**, the glossy, optimistic tech style of about 2004–2013: Windows Vista and 7, early smartphone ads, Wii menus.

The real (dark) Vivianne app UI does **not** appear. Product moments are rebuilt as Aero-style windows, pop-ups and glass panels.

## Reference posters

1. **"Tomorrow Starts GREEN"**: a dolphin leaping out of a browser window in front of a globe, a grass meadow, stacked "Untitled window" pop-ups, a glass water drop with a leaf, the recycle-bin icon.
2. **"Frutiger Zero in graphic design"**: pastel sky, tropical fish and hands cut out with white sticker outlines, a water-filled rounded headline. This is the modern take on the style, used here for typography cues only.
3. **"Aero City"**: a glass skyline reflected in water, goldfish in bubbles, butterflies, a girl reading on grass, OS window chrome and the Windows flag.

## The idea behind it

Technology and nature living in harmony. The screen and the world bleed into each other: animals swim out of windows and fish float past skyscrapers.

The mood is hopeful, clean, airy and a little naive, the future as 2007 imagined it. It fits Vivianne's message: personal AI that lives with you, in harmony with your life.

## Colour

| Role | Colour |
|------|--------|
| Main | Sky blue to cyan, white |
| Second | Fresh grass green |
| Warm accent (only one) | Goldfish orange |
| Light | White specular highlights, lens flares |

- Gradients run light at the top to deeper at the bottom, like sky or water.
- Colour is saturated and bright on near-white. Avoid muddy or dark backgrounds.

## Materials (the signature)

- **Glass and gloss:** soap bubbles, water drops, glass icons, glossy buttons, a curved highlight across the top of every object.
- **Water:** splashes, rippling fills, reflections, aquarium light, caustics.
- **Light:** lens flares, light streaks, soft bloom, sparkles on edges.
- **Photo cutouts, not illustrations:** dolphins, goldfish and clownfish, butterflies, clouds, glass skyscrapers, grass, leaves, people.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** wide, rounded, futuristic display type, often italic or extended. It has a thick outline (white or deep blue), an inner glow, and a glossy gradient or water-texture fill.
- **Supporting lines:** small, widely spaced all-caps tech sans.
- **The "system" voice:** monospace inside windows and pop-ups ("Innovating for a world that thrives in harmony.").
- **Wordmark:** small, at the top of posters.

## Interface as decoration

- Retro window chrome: blue title bars and the minimise, maximise and close buttons.
- Cascades of stacked "Untitled window" pop-ups.
- Notification toasts ("New Message!"), arrow cursors, a URL bar.
- OS icons used as props: the recycle bin, a chat bubble, the Windows flag, a globe.
- Things break out of their window frames.

## Composition

- A dense, layered collage with a lot happening at once.
- A big headline at the top, the hero subject in the centre, small UI scraps and bubbles around the edges.
- Depth comes from scale, blur, bubbles in front of and behind the subject, and reflections in water.

## The logo in this world

The tesseract mark (the redraw is in `Downloads/vivianne-logo/`) is rendered as a **glass or water-filled object**: glossy cyan with a white highlight and a soft reflection, floating in the sky or inside a bubble.

## Motion

| Pattern | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Bubbles floating and bobbing | Ambient life in every scene |
| Light sweep across glossy type | Headline reveals |
| Water ripple or splash | Scene transitions |
| Window pops open with slight overshoot, cascades | UI moments, feature reveals |
| Fish, butterflies and leaves drifting across | Connective tissue between shots |
| Slow parallax (sky, mid-ground, foreground bubbles) | Depth on held shots |
| Lens-flare pass | Logo and title moments |

## Sound

Bright, airy synth pads, soft chimes and the classic start-up and notification sounds. Optional water and nature ambience under them.
