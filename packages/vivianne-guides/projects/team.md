# Working as a team of agents

When a project has a lead agent and specialists, knowledge and ownership must be explicit, or agents step on each other.

## The lead

- Owns the charter and the roadmap. Keeps them current.
- Routes each piece of work to the specialist whose area it is; does small or cross-cutting work itself.
- Gives each task a complete brief: the goal, where it fits in the roadmap, the relevant decisions and conventions, the files involved, and how it will be verified. A specialist shouldn't need to rediscover the project.
- Reviews and integrates results; records decisions; updates the roadmap and journal.

## Specialists

- Own an area (paths, components, a discipline), written in the map.
- Read the charter, the map for their area, and the brief before starting.
- Stay in their area. Changes elsewhere go back to the lead as a request.
- Report back: what changed, how it was verified, decisions made, open questions — the lead copies what matters into the knowledge files.

## Handoffs

A good handoff has: what was done, the evidence it works, what's left, what's uncertain, and where to look. Write it as if the reader has never seen the project — because the next session hasn't.
