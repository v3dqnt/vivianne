# Layout

## Spacing scale

Use one scale and nothing in between: **4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64** (pt for print, px for screens). Space above a heading is about twice the space below it. Space between sections is larger than space between paragraphs.

## Margins

| Output | Margins |
|---|---|
| A4 / Letter report | 21 mm top, 24 mm sides, 22 mm bottom |
| Letter (correspondence) | 25–30 mm all round; text block can be narrower than the page |
| Slide 16:9 | 0.8 in sides, 0.6 in top; nothing within 0.5 in of the bottom except the footer |
| Poster | 6–8% of the short side |
| Social image | 6–8% of the width |

## Grid

- One strong left edge that titles, text, tables and images share.
- Two or three columns for dense pages; twelve-column thinking for posters and one-pagers.
- Things in a row align at their tops (or baselines for text).
- Images and charts span the text width, or a clear fraction of it (½, ⅓), never an arbitrary size.

## Page sizes

| Use | Size |
|---|---|
| A4 | 210 × 297 mm |
| US Letter | 8.5 × 11 in (215.9 × 279.4 mm) |
| A3 poster | 297 × 420 mm |
| A5 flyer / invitation | 148 × 210 mm |
| Business card | 85 × 55 mm (US 3.5 × 2 in) |
| Slide | 16:9, 13.333 × 7.5 in (338.67 × 190.5 mm) |
| Instagram post | 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) or 1080 × 1080 |
| Instagram / TikTok story | 1080 × 1920 px |
| LinkedIn / X post image | 1200 × 675 px (or 1200 × 627) |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280 × 720 px |
| OG / link preview | 1200 × 630 px |
| Presentation cover / ebook | 1600 × 2560 px |

## Composition

- Give the page one focal point. Big things are few.
- Put the most important thing top-left (or centre for posters and title slides).
- Asymmetry with alignment looks designed; symmetry everywhere looks default.
- Leave a page or slide less than full. A slide two-thirds full reads better than one crammed to the edges.
- Keep running elements (header, footer, page number) small, consistent and out of the way.
