# Typography

Type is most of the design. Get it right and very little else is needed.

## Typefaces

Vivianne embeds two open typefaces in every PDF, so documents look the same on any machine:

- **Inter** — a neutral, precise sans. The default for business documents, decks, data.
- **Source Serif 4** — a warm, literary serif. For editorial reports, letters, essays, invitations, and pull quotes in any document.

Pairings that work:

| Direction | Headings | Body | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| modern | Inter | Inter | reports, decks, proposals, product docs |
| editorial | Source Serif | Source Serif | long reads, essays, letters, research |
| classic | Source Serif | Inter | formal reports, legal, finance, academic |
| warm | Source Serif | Inter | hospitality, culture, personal |
| minimal | Inter | Inter (monochrome) | portfolios, CVs, design-led work |

Never more than two families in one document (a monospace for code is free). Don't fake styles: use real weights and italics.

For Word and PowerPoint files, fonts must exist on the reader's machine, so they use **Aptos** (Office's default) and **Georgia**.

## Scale (print, A4/Letter)

| Role | Size | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover title | 34–40 pt | 650 | tracking −0.03em, line-height 1.05 |
| Document title | 24–28 pt | 650 | tracking −0.02em |
| Heading 1 | 14–15 pt | 640 | space above ≈ 2× below |
| Heading 2 | 11–11.5 pt | 620 | |
| Label (eyebrow, table header) | 6.6–7.5 pt | 650 | UPPERCASE, tracking +0.1–0.14em |
| Body | 10–10.5 pt | 400 | line-height 1.55–1.65 |
| Caption, footnote | 7.6–8 pt | 400 | muted colour |
| Key numbers | 20–24 pt | 500–600 | tabular, tracking −0.02em |

Slides scale up: titles 27–30 pt, points 18–20 pt, big numbers 50–60 pt (light weight looks premium at that size), labels 8–10 pt.

## Rules

- **Line length**: 60–85 characters for body text. On A4 with ~24 mm side margins at 10–10.5 pt, that's right. Wider pages get columns or larger margins.
- **Leading**: body 1.55–1.65; headings 1.1–1.3; big display type 1.0–1.1.
- **Tracking**: tighten large type (−0.02 to −0.035em); loosen small caps and labels (+0.1em); leave body alone.
- **Numerals**: tabular, lining figures in tables and numbers that are compared.
- **Case**: sentence case for titles and headings. UPPERCASE only for small labels, always tracked out.
- **Emphasis**: bold for a few key words; italic for titles of works and a light touch. Never underline except links.
- **Punctuation**: real quotes (“ ” ‘ ’), en dash for ranges (2024–2026), em dash for breaks — like this, ellipsis …, × for dimensions, minus − for negatives in tables.
- **Widows and orphans**: avoid a single word on the last line of a paragraph or a heading alone at the bottom of a page.
- **Hyphenation**: on in justified or narrow columns; off for headings.
