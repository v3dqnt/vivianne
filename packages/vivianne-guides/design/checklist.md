# Before you hand it over

Look at the preview Vivianne attaches (first page or slide) and check:

**Content**
- [ ] The title says what the document is for, and the first thing on the page is the most important.
- [ ] No placeholder, filler, or repeated text. Names, dates and numbers are right.

**Type and space**
- [ ] Nothing overflows, is cut off, or wraps awkwardly (a single word on a line, a heading alone at a page bottom).
- [ ] Headings, captions and labels are consistent across the document.
- [ ] Margins and spacing are even; the page isn't crowded.

**Colour and emphasis**
- [ ] The accent is used in a few places only. Text is near-black.
- [ ] Nothing is emphasised that doesn't need to be.

**Data**
- [ ] Numbers are right-aligned with their headers; rounding is sensible and consistent.
- [ ] Charts have a clear lead series and a title that says the finding.

**Slides**
- [ ] One idea per slide; ≤ 5 points; nothing near the edges.
- [ ] Title slide, section slides and the closing slide feel like one family.

**Files**
- [ ] Word/PowerPoint/Excel: it opens, and the user can edit it naturally (real headings, real lists, live formulas).
- [ ] The file is named for the reader (`Q4 Board Report.pdf`), not `output.pdf`.

If anything fails, fix it and render again before saying it's done.
