# Vivianne guides

How Vivianne works, written down: the taste behind the documents she makes, how she draws science in chat, and the way she gets to know and carry a project. Open source, so you can read how she decides, change it, or point her at your own version.

Vivianne reads these through her `guide` tool, and only the parts a task needs. She keeps a local copy refreshed from GitHub at most once a day, and a copy built into the app for when she's offline. Any agent can use them the same way: they're plain Markdown.

## Contents

**[design/](design/index.md)** — documents that look designed, not generated.
Principles, typography, colour, layout, data, writing, per-format guides (PDF, slides, Word, Excel, graphics), a pre-delivery checklist, and HTML/CSS templates.

**[science/](science/index.md)** — chemistry, biology, physics and maths drawn in chat.
Equations (`\ce`, `\pu`), structures and reactions, 3D proteins, sequences, plasmid maps, trees, pathways, cells, graphs with sliders, free-body diagrams, optics, circuits, waves; the syntax of each fence, worked examples, and the `check_diagram` tool that validates a script before it is sent.

**[projects/](projects/index.md)** — knowing the thing being built.
What to do when a new project starts, when joining one that already exists, how to keep a living picture of the product and its objectives, and how to work so the next agent (or the next session) can pick up where you left off.

## Use your own

Fork this repository, change what's yours — brand colours, typefaces, tone, templates, the way your team works — and set **Settings → Mods → Guides** to your fork. Vivianne will follow it instead.
